## 3. Joint positions, coordinates, hierarchy (Tasks 1–2)
### 3.1 Mesh / index correspondence (MEASURED)
`base.obj`: 19,158 vertices, 18,486 faces (all quads), 139 groups. Group `body` = exactly vertices 0…13379 (13,380; 13,378 faces). Then `helper-tongue` (13380–13605), 125 `joint-*` cube groups of 8 vertices each (1,000 vertices, indices 13606…19157, shared with no body vertex), eyes/teeth/lashes/genital/tights/skirt/hair helpers. Both `weights.default.json` and `weights.game_engine.json` cover vertex indices 0…19157 (all 19,158); Rigify weights cover 19,142 (the 16 missing are two joint cubes, 13622–13629 and 19150–19157). Vertex indices used by rig `vertex_index(es)` are 0-based (1-based variant gives larger error).

### 3.2 Coordinates and frames
* `rig = (x, −z, y)/10` (OBJ Y-up decimetres → Z-up metres) has determinant +1 (a +90° rotation about X, then ÷10). MEASURED.
* Body extent in rig space: x ±0.496, y −0.321…+0.102, z −0.8168…+0.8491 → body height **1.6659 m**. Raw OBJ units are dm (height 16.66), matching Rigify `scale_factor 0.1`.
* Side: all `.L` bones with non-zero x have head x>0 (default 62/62, game_engine 23/23, Rigify 47/47); `.R` all x<0. Front −Y: toe end y −0.191 vs ankle y +0.001; eyes and breasts y < spine y. MEASURED.
* **Frames differ between rig families (MEASURED):** `default` and `game_engine` use the mesh frame (origin ≈ pelvis level). Both Rigify rigs are shifted by **+0.8183 m in Z** (ground at z=0; median VERTEX-joint offset 0.8237, = −z of cube `joint-ground`). 
* **Anomaly:** `game_engine` bone `Root` has `default_position (0,0,0)` but its cube `joint-ground` is at z=−0.8183 in the mesh frame (error 0.8183 m on that one bone).

### 3.3 Recomputing joint heads from the mesh (Task 1) — does not reproduce `default_position`
Strategies (counts, default rig, head+tail): CUBE 216, VERTEX 84, MEAN 26. Cube centre = mean of 8 vertices (mean vs bbox centre differ ≤0.11 mm). Rigify adds `XYZ` (x/y/z from three vertices; 4 ends) and `offset` (3 bones).

By strategy (default rig): VERTEX median 0.96 cm (head)/0.95 cm (tail), MEAN ≈2.2 cm, CUBE ≈3.9 cm; hand/finger ends median 5.8 cm vs other bones 1.7 cm. ### 3.5 Foot / sole (re-measured from body vertices only)
* Foot bone pitch below horizontal: default `foot.L` **27.2°** (default_position) / 25.2° (mesh-recomputed); game_engine `foot_l` 27.2° / 28.2°, length 0.132 m; `ball_l` −2.7° (flat, i.e. +2.7° up). 
* Sole plane (288 lowest left-foot body vertices, 0.232 m along the foot): slope **0.52° (toe slightly higher; |slope| ≈ 0.5°, same magnitude as the earlier chat figure, which used the opposite sign convention)**. Ankle height above the sole plane: **6.75 cm** (mesh-recomputed joint), 6.37 cm (default_position), 7.20 cm above the single lowest vertex. The earlier 9.6 cm figure included non-body helper geometry (all-vertex min z −0.8449) and is superseded.
* **New:** the game_engine `foot_l` tail (ball) lands within 1–3 mm of the sole plane (tail height/ankle height = 0.015–0.046). The pitch is therefore just `atan(ankle height / horizontal ankle→ball)` = 28.8–29.0° — a consequence of proportions over a flat sole, not a sloped sole. The default-rig `foot.L` (ankle→mid-foot) ends 3.4–3.7 cm above the sole and would need 46–48° to reach it.

## 5. Weight falloff across joints (Task 4b)
Method (MEASURED, `05_weight_falloff.py`): joint centre and limb axis come from the `base.obj` cubes (shoulder→elbow, elbow→hand, upper-leg→knee, knee→ankle) so weights and geometry share one frame. Body vertices (0…13379) on the same side, within a cylinder around the axis (radius shoulder 10 cm, elbow 7, hip 14, knee 9; t from −15 to +20 cm), are binned by t (1 cm). `w_dist` = summed weight of all bones distal to the joint (subtree by parent links; Rigify: metarig subtree + `DEF-*.001`). Blend zone = 10 %→90 % crossing of the bin mean; t=0 is the joint centre, + toward the limb end. Left side shown; the right side agrees within 0.35 cm on t50/t90 (mesh is symmetric), except the knee t10 (`knee_r` −2.3 cm vs −3.5 cm left) — see `weight_falloff_summary.json` for both sides.

### 6.2 Method
Landmarks (INFERRED pairing): hip = thigh head / `joint-l-upper-leg`; knee; ankle; toe tip = foot tail / `joint-l-toe-2-4`; shoulder = upper_arm head; elbow; wrist; hand tip; skull base = head head / `joint-head`; skull top = head tail / body top vertex. Stature S = sole → skull top. MPFB2 values are given three ways (body top; cube `joint-head-2`; `default_position` joints from game_engine) to show the mesh-vs-default uncertainty. **Only pose-independent quantities are compared** (3D segment lengths, ratios, hip spacing, ankle height, head length); arm/shoulder/ankle lateral positions are pose-dependent (arms 90.5° from vertical in v8.3, 11.3° in v10, 47.9° in MPFB2; MPFB2 legs splay 7.8°, Star Rai legs vertical) and are written to `compare_landmarks_normalised.csv` but NOT used for conclusions.

## 8. Transferable vs incompatible ideas (Task 6)
**Potentially transferable (principles):**
1. *Mesh-driven joint placement*: define each joint by a vertex group/vertices of the mesh (cube centre, single vertex, mean of two), not stored coordinates, so a rebuilt body re-derives the rig (113 of 163 default heads are CUBE). Caveat: MPFB2's own cached `default_position` drifts 1–6 cm from the raw mesh, so a pipeline must always recompute from the live mesh and treat caches as hints.
2. *Foot bone ends on the sole plane over a flat sole*; pitch = atan(ankle height / ankle→ball distance) and is a proportion consequence (27–29° MPFB2; ~45° at Star Rai proportions). Foot pitch itself is not a sole-slope requirement.
3. *Two-tier weighting*: split limbs at mean-of-two-vertices into 01/02 bones for smooth twist; a low-influence game variant (mean 1.85, 95 % ≤4) derived from the rich set at identical blend positions — a useful template for a mobile/Live2D-friendly weight set.
4. *Blend-zone widths as fractions of segment length* (shoulder ≈ 0.68, hip ≈ 0.48, elbow ≈ 0.31, knee ≈ 0.24 of the following segment) give an anatomical starting range for Star Rai weight painting, to be rescaled by segment length (anime segments are shorter relative to stature).
5. *Masks as separate vertex groups* (`mhmask-preserve-volume`, `mhmask-no-smooth`) kept apart from skin weights.
**Incompatible / not usable directly:** MPFB2 joint coordinates and weights (realistic ~11-head body, A-pose, different topology — vertex indices have no meaning for Star Rai meshes); Rigify metarig data (requires Rigify generation, 6 parentless roots, constraints); the `.L = +X`, front −Y convention vs v10 (+Y, left −X) needs the explicit (−x,−y,z) mapping; arm and lateral landmarks (pose-dependent); game_engine `Root` default_position (0,0,0) vs `joint-ground` at −0.818 m.

## 9. Limitations (honest list)
* Cylinder-based falloff selects body vertices near the limb axis; neighbouring structures (e.g. torso behind the shoulder at t<0) enter the proximal plateau; the mesh is the raw base, not the shaped default human.
* Rigify falloff widths are not comparable to standard rigs (helper-bone weights).
* Star Rai landmark pairing and sole planes are INFERRED from bone JSON only; no Star Rai mesh was opened.
* `default_position` ↔ raw mesh mismatch cause is NOT VERIFIABLE with the approved files.
* No Blender evaluation of deformation (no Blender allowed); weights are analysed statically.
