# v10 limited wave v1: the retargeted `greet_02` held inside the measured printed-joint limits — method and criteria (FROZEN before any result)

Issue #177. **Research only**, rig side: no part geometry is changed and no redesign is proposed here. Evaluator: Claude. Frozen 2026-11-12. The v10 files are read from copies and are not modified or committed. No GLB, .blend, STL or Tripo-derived pose is committed.

## Question

The joint-range check found that 10 of 15 assessed v10 joints cannot bend as far as the retargeted wave asks. What does the wave look like if every assessed joint is held to the bend the printed parts allow, and what does it lose?

## Method

For every key t (135 keys):

1. **Joints limited:** the 15 joints assessed in `joint_range.json`, each a (parent bone p, child bone c) pair: neck (`upper_chest`, `head`); spine (`hip`, `lower_waist`), (`lower_waist`, `waist`), (`waist`, `mid_chest`), (`mid_chest`, `upper_chest`); and per side hip (`pelvis`, `thigh`), elbow (`upper_arm`, `lower_arm`), wrist (`lower_arm`, `hand`), knee (`thigh`, `shin`), ankle (`shin`, `foot`).
2. **Relative rotation:** R_rel(t) = Q_p(t)ᵀ · Q_c(t), from the input pose. This is the rotation `joint_range.py` tested.
3. **Fraction:** s = s*(t) for that joint from `joint_range.json`. If s = 1 the joint is unchanged. If s < 1, s′ = max(0, s − 0.001). The 0.001 keeps s′ below the tested clear value (the published s is rounded to 4 decimals; one bisection step is 1/1024).
4. **Limited rotation:** R′_rel(t) = the rotation about the same axis as R_rel(t) with its angle multiplied by s′ (the same scaling the joint-range test used).
5. **Rebuild, parents first:** Q′_pelvis = Q_pelvis. For every other bone c with nearest bone ancestor p: Q′_c = Q′_p · R′_rel if (p, c) is a limited joint, otherwise Q′_c = Q′_p · (Q_pᵀ Q_c), which keeps that joint's original relative rotation. Attachment bones take their parent's Q′.
6. **Not limited (no data):** the shoulders (`upper_chest` → `upper_arm`) and `pelvis` → `hip`. They keep their original relative rotation.
7. **Positions:** forward kinematics as in the retarget; root translation unchanged; no bone is scaled.

Each key is treated on its own; nothing is smoothed.

## Criteria

| | Rule |
|---|---|
| **L1** | Bone lengths: abs(length − rest length) ≤ 0.01 mm for the 19 bones at every key. |
| **L2** | Within limits: at every key, for each of the 15 joints, the limited relative angle is ≤ s*(t) · θ(t) (the published allowed angle, with 0.01° numerical tolerance), and where s*(t) = 1 the relative rotation equals the input's (≤ 1e-6°). |
| **L3** | Hand reach, same rule as retarget C2: in the raised part (the same 22 keys, 3.042–3.917 s), at one or more keys the highest point of `hand_right` is ≥ 116 mm and ≥ the z of the `head` bone's head at that key. |
| **L4** | Skeleton clearance, same rule as retarget C3: capsule radius 1.2 mm, surface clearance ≥ 2 mm between segments of different groups, same exclusions; rest pose must pass. |

PASS or FAIL per criterion; no combined verdict. Nothing is changed after results; any change is a new frozen amendment.

## Reported, not gated

- Per joint: largest relative angle before and after; number of keys changed.
- Right hand: highest point and wrist height in the raised part, before and after; largest distance of the right wrist and hand tip from their input positions.
- Feet: ankle and foot-tail heights before and after (limiting hips and knees moves the feet; nothing re-plants them).
- Head: largest change in the direction the `head` bone points.
- Smoothness: for each limited joint, the largest key-to-key change of the relative rotation, before and after (per-key limiting can introduce steps).
- Lower bound: per joint, the largest pure-swing angle of the input pose next to the published allowed angle.
- Renders: front and side, input (A1) and limited, at the keys nearest 0.04, 2.20, 3.00, 3.40, 3.80 and 5.00 s.

## Not covered

- **Clearance between printed parts in the limited pose.** Each joint's limit comes from a test of that joint alone; whether limbs and body parts clear each other in the new pose needs the part-clearance test with the STL. L4 is skeleton-only.
- Shoulders: not limited, because their range was not assessed.
- Whether the limited wave looks acceptable.
- Contact inside a joint between keys.

## Reproducibility

Python 3, numpy only. Run twice; the result JSON must be byte-identical. The limited pose file is hashed and delivered to the user, not committed. Per-key criterion values are committed.
