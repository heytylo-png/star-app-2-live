Star Rai — idle blink composite (no full-PNG clip)

Problem
Blink flash = two full PNGs clipping / crossfading over each other.
Stop that. Body must not punch.

Composite only (authoritative steps)
1. Register blink sheet onto the LIVE idle canvas (public/rai/idle.png, 1008×1792).
   Scale/letterbox first if the blink source is 720×1280 (or any other size).
2. Cut the same two eye holes from glare (live idle) and from the registered blink
   (same x,y,w,h per eye for both sheets).
3. Paste blink holes into the glare body. Produce 2–4 frames. Restore glare eyes
   after the closed-lid hold (back to open glare eyes).
4. Do not cut hair, ahoge, mouth, or collar. Stay on idle key only.

Runtime
- Do not opacity-crossfade / stack two full-body PNGs for blink.
- Animate using the composite frames (glare body + eye holes only), or an
  equivalent eye-region composite on the live idle key.
- Rest/idle only; cancel mid-blink on pose/talk/tint; prefers-reduced-motion off.
- Named poses do not blink.

Assets
- Live glare/idle key: public/rai/idle.png (1008×1792) — never replace this as default.
- Blink source: public/rai/idle_blink.png (or official closed-lid still registered to that canvas).
- Ship baked composites (and/or replace idle_blink.png with a body-locked composite)
  so outside the two eye holes the pixels match live idle (max delta 0).
