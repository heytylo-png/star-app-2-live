V1 BEHAVIOR
- User can name a track / paste a title.
  That sets memory: now_playing + session_on (Life session active).
- “what are you listening to” / similar ask:
  → one beat if daily_playlist OR now_playing exists
  → else she does NOT invent a concert / setlist
- In-session: one comment per track change.
  Voice card length (1–2 short sentences).
  Prefer pose: talk | content | smug | tired | wave
- Daily playlist: 4–8 titles kept in memory.
  Do not recite the list.
  Do not announce it in the morning.
- Mood tag: bratty | smug | tired | soft.
  HERS for the local day — not a user picker.
  Set once per local day from Chart/clock/sky + chat, or when she comments on music.
  Tint wording only. Not a new personality. Read-only on Life.
- She may suggest a track into the Daily list / Spotify queue
  (Grok or local brain when the Life session is on, or when the user asks).
  Suggestions show on Life. Optional “Play suggestion” uses Spotify search/play if connected.
  Suggesting is not a track change. One Chat comment only when the track actually changes.
- She keeps her own favorite lists (named title lists she likes).
  Local titles only. User can browse on Life. Play still goes through Spotify if connected.
  Not user-curated lists pretending to be hers. Grok may seed/update from listening + voice.
